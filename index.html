Quick Reference & Module Functions Guide

🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy

Your audio files are never uploaded to any cloud server. All audio decoding, Web Worker FFT signal processing, and rendering happen locally inside your browser. Unreleased tracks stay 100% secure on your computer.

1. Control Bar & Routing

Load audio via file selection or full-page drag & drop. Toggle playback with Spacebar. Select Left Only or Right Only to isolate channels, or turn Mono: ON to audition mono-compatibility.

2. Stereo Waveform Overview

Visualizes track amplitude over time. Bar colors reveal phase health: Green = In-Phase, Yellow = Wide Stereo, and Red = Out-of-Phase / Anti-Phase (risk of mono cancellation).

3. Goniometer / Phase Scope

Shows 2D stereo soundfield distribution and phase correlation meter. Switch between Scope: Raw Track View (unfiltered stereo audio file) and Scope: Post Mono ON Routing View (monitored signal that collapses visually into a straight vertical line in mono mode).

4. Spectrum Analyzer (RTA) & Level Meter

Real-time Float32 frequency response curves and Stereo Peak Level meter (0 dBFS to -90 dBFS). Features a single common middle linear dB scale, named PEAK dBFS header, customizable RTA display boost (+0 dB to +18 dB), peak-hold indicators, and numerical dB readouts.

5. Interactive Spectrogram

Ultra-HD 8192-FFT dual-channel spectral visualizer with Blackman-Harris windowing (-92 dB sidelobe attenuation) and Bilinear Anti-Aliased Rendering. Features Mel & Linear frequency scaling, 64x zoom, variable gain boost, and crosshair frequency/gain-dBFS/timestamp reading.

Studio Mix & Frequency Spectrum Quick Reference Sheet

Band Name Frequency Range Spectral Characteristics & Sonic Function Common Fixes & Mix Guidelines
Sub-Bass 20 Hz - 60 Hz Sub-bass feel, physical rumble, sub-kick energy. Highly prone to phase cancellation in mono. High-pass side channel at 100 Hz to keep sub-bass 100% mono centered.
Bass & Low-End 60 Hz - 250 Hz Root note fundamentals of bass instruments, kick drum body, warmth and punch. Duck bass around kick transient fundamental frequency to avoid muddy accumulation.
Low-Mids 250 Hz - 500 Hz Body of vocals, snare weight, acoustic guitar power. Excessive energy causes boxiness. Dip narrow notch around 300-400 Hz if mix sounds muddy or enclosed.
Mid-Range 500 Hz - 2 kHz Vocal presence, lead synths, horn body, horn fundamentals. Human ear is extremely sensitive here. Maintain balanced energy distribution to preserve instrument intelligibility in mono.
Upper-Mids 2 kHz - 4 kHz Attack transients, vocal edge, pick noise, snare snap. Harshness builds quickly around 3 kHz. Dynamic EQ cut at 3 kHz if vocals sound overly biting or fatiguing.
Presence & Brilliance 4 kHz - 20 kHz Cymbal sizzle, air, shimmer, reverb tails, upper harmonic air frequencies. Use smooth high-shelf boost on vocals above 10 kHz for silkiness.
